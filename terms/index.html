“You” means the business that signs up for the service, and anyone you authorise to act for it.

2. What you’re buying

One flat monthly service, everything included. Specifically:

  • A website — built for your business, hosted by us, and kept up to date while you’re a client.
  • Social posts — roughly twenty posts a month, written and designed for your business and published to the Instagram and Facebook accounts you connect.
  • Your Google Business listing — we keep the information on it accurate (hours, phone number, website, address, category), ask your customers for reviews, and draft replies to the reviews you receive.
  • A monthly recap — a plain summary of what actually went out.

We do not post to Google Business. The Google part of the service is your listing and your reviews, not a posting feed. We mention it because the difference is easy to assume and we’d rather be clear about it up front.

3. The free two weeks

New clients get fourteen days free before anything is charged. You can start with a card on file or without one — both routes exist and both are real.

If you started with a card and do nothing, the trial rolls into a paid month automatically on day fifteen. If you cancel before then, you are charged nothing at all. If you started without a card, nothing happens automatically; we’ll ask you before any money changes hands.

4. Price and billing

$100 per month. There is no setup fee. There is no contract, no minimum term, and no cancellation fee.

Billing is monthly in advance and renews automatically until you cancel. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see, handle, or store your full card number — Stripe does, and you can read their privacy policy.

If we ever change the price, existing clients get at least 30 days’ notice by email before it takes effect, and you can cancel in that window without paying the new rate.

Referral credit

If you refer another business that becomes a paying client, $50 a month comes off your bill for as long as they stay active. It stacks — two active referrals bring a $100 plan to $0. Credit is applied to your subscription, not paid out in cash, and it can reduce your bill to zero but never below it.

5. Cancelling

Cancel in any month you like. No notice period, no cancellation fee, and no phone call where we try to keep you. Full detail — including how to do it and what happens afterwards — is on the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

6. What we need from you

The service only works if we can actually do the work, so:

  • Accurate information. Your real hours, phone number, address, services and prices. We publish what you tell us; if it’s wrong, what we publish is wrong.
  • Access to post. You connect your Instagram, Facebook and Google Business accounts so we can publish on your behalf. You can disconnect at any time — it’s your account, and revoking access is always in your hands, not ours.
  • The right to use what you give us. When you send us photos, a logo, or copy, you’re confirming you own it or have permission to use it. We can’t verify that for you, and we rely on it being true.
  • A reply now and then. Some things — a review that needs a real answer, a factual correction — need you. We’ll keep it to a minimum.

7. Who owns what

You own your side. Your business name and brand, your social accounts, your Google listing, your customer relationships, the photos and copy you gave us, and the content we produced for your business. If you leave, that content is still yours to keep and use.

We own our side. The software that runs this service, our templates, our designs before they’re applied to your business, and our internal processes. Those aren’t transferred by paying for the service.

If you cancel, ask and we’ll send you the files for your website so you can host it elsewhere. We won’t hold your own material hostage.

8. What we don’t promise

We do not guarantee results. Not a number of customers, not a number of leads, not a sale, not a position in Google search results, not a follower count, and not a revenue figure.

Marketing is not a product with a guaranteed outcome, and any company that tells you otherwise is selling you something we’re not. What we guarantee is the work: the site gets built, the posts get made and published, the listing gets kept accurate, and you get an honest monthly account of exactly what happened — including a month where the answer is “less than we’d like.”

9. Platforms we don’t control

We publish through Instagram, Facebook and Google. Those are their platforms, running under their rules, and they change them without asking us. If one of them suspends an account, rejects a post, changes what its API allows, or removes a feature we were using, that is outside our control.

What we owe you when it happens is honesty and effort: we’ll tell you promptly, explain what actually broke, and find the way around it if there is one. We can’t promise a platform will keep behaving.

10. Things we won’t publish

We can decline work, or end the service, if we’re asked to produce or publish:

  • anything illegal, or anything promoting illegal activity;
  • claims about your business we know to be false — a licence you don’t hold, a warranty you don’t offer, a certification you don’t have;
  • false or disparaging claims about a competitor;
  • harassment, hate speech, or content targeting a private individual;
  • adult content, or content aimed at children in a way we think is inappropriate;
  • fake reviews, incentivised reviews that break platform rules, or anything designed to mislead a customer about who is speaking.

This isn’t a moral posture — those things get accounts banned and businesses sued, and we’re not putting your listing at that risk or ours.
