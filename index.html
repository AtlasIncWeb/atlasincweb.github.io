Straight answers

The questions every owner asks us.

If something here isn’t answered plainly enough, call and ask. We’d rather talk you out of it now than disappoint you later.

What exactly will you post to my Instagram?

Your work, your people, and the things you’d tell a customer standing in front of you — the 6am ovens, the tip you give every client, the story of the corner you’ve been on for years. Four or five posts a week, in your colors and your voice. Never a wall of discounts.

Do I get to see it before it goes out?

Yes. Nothing publishes in your name that you haven’t seen. And once a post goes out, Atlas checks that it actually published — if it didn’t land, it gets caught and re-run instead of quietly vanishing.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Posting access is granted through Instagram and Facebook’s own business tools — your password stays yours, and you can revoke our access whenever you want without changing a thing about your login.

Am I locked in?

No contract, ever. Two weeks free, then $100 a month, month to month. Cancel in any month you like: no notice period, no cancellation fee, no phone call where we try to keep you.

What if I don’t like a post?

Say so and it changes — it’s your name on it, so it’s your call. If the whole look is off, we rebuild the look. That is exactly what the free two weeks are for.

How much of my time does this take?

Five minutes to start. After that: a glance at what we’ve made, and a photo texted over when you happen to take a good one. That’s the entire job on your end.

What does the $100 actually cover?

The website, the hosting, the posts, the Google profile, and the monthly recap. All of it. No setup fee, no per-post charge, no “that’s the premium tier.” If we ever add something, it won’t arrive as a new line on your bill.

Why should I trust a company I haven’t heard of?

You shouldn’t — not on a promise. That is precisely why the first two weeks are free and why there’s no contract: you see the real site and the real posts before any money changes hands, and you can walk at any point. Judge the work, not the pitch.

What Atlas doesn’t do

  • We don’t buy followers or write reviews for you.
  • We don’t run discount blasts or coupon spam.
  • We don’t promise a #1 Google ranking — nobody honest can.
  • We don’t manage ad spend; the $100 buys work, not ads.
  • We don’t ask for a year up front, or any contract at all.
  • We don’t hand you off to a sales team. There isn’t one.

If one of those is what you were hoping for, we’re the wrong shop — and we’d rather say so now than take your money and find out in month three.
