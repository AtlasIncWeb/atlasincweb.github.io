Refunds & Cancellation

How to leave, and what you get back.

The sales page says “cancel any month, no cancellation fee” in seven different places. This page is the part that has to be true — exactly how you cancel, what happens next, and when money comes back.

Effective 3 August 2026 · Last updated 3 August 2026

Before you pay anything: two weeks free

Every new client gets fourteen days free. The site gets built, posts go out, and you see the actual work before a single dollar moves.

If you started with a card on file and cancel before day fifteen, you are charged nothing at all — not a partial month, not a fee, nothing. If you started without a card, there is nothing to cancel and nothing to refund; we simply stop.

How to cancel

Three ways, all of them equally valid. None of them involves being talked out of it.

1. Do it yourself, without talking to us

Go to our homepage and click “Manage your plan” at the bottom. Enter the email address on your account and we’ll send you a link to your billing portal — a page hosted by Stripe where you cancel with one button, change your card, or download invoices. Nobody at Atlas needs to action anything.

Every monthly recap we send you points at that same page, so you never have to go looking for it.

For your security, that link is only ever emailed to the address already on your account — never to whatever address is typed into the form. So nobody else can request it on your behalf.

2. Email

Write to atlasinc.web@gmail.com and say you want to cancel. You don’t need to explain why, and you won’t be asked to. We’ll action it and confirm in writing.

3. Phone

(401) 317-4668. Same result, same day.

There is no notice period and no cancellation fee. There is no form to complete, no exit survey, and no requirement to speak to anyone.

When it takes effect

Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you’ve already paid for. You keep the full service until then — posts keep going out, the site stays up, the listing stays maintained — and you are not billed again.

You’re never cut off mid-month for cancelling. You paid for that month; you get that month.

If you’d rather stop immediately instead of running out the month, say so and we’ll stop immediately — see the refund note below.

Refunds

What we refund in full, always

No argument, no proration, no waiting:

  • You were charged during the free trial.
  • You were charged after you cancelled.
  • You were charged twice for the same month.
  • You were charged the wrong amount, including a referral credit we owed you and didn’t apply.
  • We didn’t do the work. If a month went by and the posts didn’t go out, the site went down and stayed down, or the listing went unmaintained, that month is refunded. You shouldn’t have to ask, and if our own monthly recap shows a month like that, we’ll raise it before you do.

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within five to ten business days once we’ve issued them — the exact timing is your bank’s, not ours.

What we don’t automatically refund

A month you used. If the work was delivered and you simply decided to stop, we don’t refund the month already in progress — you keep the service through to the end of it instead. That’s why the two free weeks exist: so the decision to pay comes after you’ve seen the work, not before.

If you ask us to stop immediately rather than run out the month, we’ll stop immediately, but the month already paid isn’t refunded — you’re choosing to end service you’ve already bought.

When we’re the ones ending it

If we stop serving your account for any reason other than the conduct listed in section 10 of the Terms of Service, you get at least 30 days’ notice and a refund of the unused part of the month, prorated to the day.

If none of the above fits

Tell us what happened. We’re a small business in Warwick, not a support queue, and a real person will read it. We’d rather refund a month and part on decent terms than win an argument with somebody who tells other business owners about it.

Coming back

If you cancel and later want to return, just ask. There’s no penalty and no re-onboarding fee — though the free trial is once per business, so a returning client starts paying from day one.

Failed payments

If a card fails we’ll email you and try again over the following days. We won’t silently stop working or silently delete anything, and we won’t hammer your card repeatedly. If it still can’t be collected we’ll pause the account and tell you plainly that we’ve done so, with a date, before anything comes down.
