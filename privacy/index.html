Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what we do with it.

Short version: we collect what we need to do the work, we don’t sell it, and this website doesn’t track you at all. The long version is below, including the actual list of companies your information passes through.

Effective 3 August 2026 · Last updated 3 August 2026

Who’s responsible for your data

Atlas Inc., a trade name used by a sole proprietorship in Warwick, Rhode Island. 145 Essex Rd, Building B, Warwick, RI 02888. Questions, corrections, or deletion requests go to atlasinc.web@gmail.com or (401) 317-4668, and a real person answers them.

This website doesn’t track you

No analytics. No advertising pixels. No cookies. No local storage. No session recording. No third-party scripts of any kind.

That’s not a promise about the future written vaguely enough to be technically true — it’s a description of the page you are reading right now, which loads no external code whatsoever. There is no cookie banner on this site because there is nothing to consent to.

The one thing we can’t see but should still tell you about: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub’s servers keep their own standard web logs, including visitor IP addresses, as part of running the service. We don’t receive, request, or have access to those logs. GitHub’s handling of them is covered by their privacy statement.

What we collect

If you contact us or sign up

The form on our site asks for three things: your business name, your email address, and anything you choose to type in the comments box. That’s all of it. If you call or email instead, we have whatever you tell us.

If you become a client

To build a website and run social accounts for a business, we hold:

  • business name, category, city and address;
  • phone number, email address and website;
  • your hours, services, offers and prices — the facts we publish on your behalf;
  • brand material you give us: logo, colours, tone of voice, tagline;
  • photographs you send us;
  • authorisation to publish to the social accounts you connect;
  • a Stripe customer reference, your plan price, and your billing status.

We never see or store your card number. Card details go directly to Stripe and stay there. What we hold is a reference identifier and whether you’re paid up.

We also don’t hold your social media passwords. Posting access works through a connection you authorise and can revoke yourself at any time, from your own account settings, without asking us.

If we contacted you first

We do cold outreach to local businesses, so you may hear from us before you’ve ever heard of us. In that case we hold publicly listed business information — business name, category, address, phone number, website, and public Google rating and review count — gathered from the Google Places API and OpenStreetMap. Business contact details, not personal ones, and nothing that wasn’t already published for customers to find.

Every outreach email carries a real postal address and a working unsubscribe. Reply “stop” or email maya@atlasincteam.com and you’re removed — permanently, not for ninety days. If you’d rather we deleted the record entirely instead of suppressing it, ask and we will.

We do not sell your information, and we never have. We don’t share it with advertisers, data brokers, or list vendors. It goes to the companies above because the work can’t happen otherwise, and nowhere else — except where the law genuinely requires it, which so far has never happened.

Content written by AI

We’ll say this plainly because you deserve to know how the work is made: posts, page copy and draft review replies are written with AI assistance, using your business facts as the input. A human reviews what goes out, and we run automated checks that refuse to publish claims your business hasn’t actually made — a licence you don’t hold, a warranty you don’t offer, a guarantee you never gave.

Your information is used to produce your content. It is not used to train anyone’s AI models.

How long we keep it

  • While you’re a client — as long as the account is active.
  • After you leave — we keep billing and tax records for seven years, because tax law requires it. The rest we’ll delete on request.
  • Prospects we contacted — if you opt out, we keep the minimum needed to make sure we never contact you again. That record exists to protect you, not to market to you. Ask for full deletion instead and we’ll do that.
  • Published work — posts already on your own accounts belong to you and stay there unless you remove them.

Your rights

Whoever you are and wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you or your business;
  • correct it if it’s wrong;
  • delete it, subject only to records we’re legally required to keep;
  • stop contacting you, immediately and permanently;
  • send you a copy of the material you gave us.

We don’t gate these behind a jurisdiction test. If you’re somewhere with specific statutory rights — California, the EU, anywhere else — we’ll honour those too. Email atlasinc.web@gmail.com; we aim to answer within 30 days and usually much sooner.

Keeping it safe

Access is limited to the owner and the systems described above. Payment details never reach our systems in the first place, which is the strongest protection available for the most sensitive thing you’d hand us. Connections to your social accounts are token-based and revocable by you.

No one can honestly promise perfect security, and we won’t. What we will promise: if a breach ever affects your information, we’ll tell you what happened, when, and what to do about it — promptly and without spin.

Children

This is a service sold to businesses. It isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe we have, tell us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects clients, we’ll email you before it takes effect. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version.
